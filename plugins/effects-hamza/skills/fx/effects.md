# Effects list

Each effect renders a 3.4 s vertical clip (1080x1920, 30 fps) from your video.
`cutout` = needs background removal (RVM, a GPU is recommended).

| id | name | group | cutout | what it does |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `orbit-ring` | Orbit ring | Cutout | yes | Letters spin around your head, passing behind and in front of you. |
| `digital-zoom` | Digital zoom | Camera | no | Hard punch into a tight face crop with a camera viewfinder. |
| `paper-tear` | Paper tear | Transition | no | A newspaper page rips in half to reveal the video. |
| `light-rays` | Light rays | Cutout | yes | Light rays burst from behind you. |
| `outline-glow` | Outline glow | Cutout | yes | A glowing line traces around you. |

| `glitch` | Glitch | Transition | no | A short digital glitch hit. |
